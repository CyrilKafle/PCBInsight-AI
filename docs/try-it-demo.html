How the score is computed

subscore = 100 − Σ (severity points × confidence)

Severity points: critical 25, high 15, medium 8, low 3, info 1. Overall is the plain mean of the seven rounded subscores. Decoupling and ground findings roll into Power. Weighting by confidence, rather than a hard cutoff, keeps uncertain findings from being either ignored or counted in full. The confidence values are set by hand per rule by the rule's author, not calibrated against labelled boards.

Confidence is a fixed per-rule heuristic from 0 to 1 that estimates how likely a finding is to be real. It is not a statistical probability. Below 0.5, a finding is treated as unverified and should be checked by hand in the CAD tool; 0.5 or above is not tagged.

PWR-001 medium power confidence 0.65

Thin power trace on net +3V3 (0.25mm)

+3V3

Why it matters

A trace this narrow limits current-carrying capacity and increases IR drop along the supply path, which can starve downstream components under load.

Size power trace width to the expected current, not the minimum fab rule.

Suggested fix

Widen this trace to at least 0.30mm, more for high-current rails.

DECAP-001 medium decoupling confidence 0.50

Decoupling capacitor C1 is 3.2mm from U1

U1C1+3V3

Why it matters

The nearest capacitor bridging +3V3 to ground is farther than recommended for a MCU, lengthening the loop the transient current has to travel and reducing the capacitor's effectiveness at high frequency.

Decoupling capacitors should sit as close as possible to the pin they protect.

Suggested fix

Move C1 within 3.0mm of U1, or add a closer one.

Recurring Theme: Possible Dangling Trace Stubs

Six of the eight findings (RTE-001 through RTE-006) share the identical pattern — a trace endpoint that doesn't clearly terminate at a pad, via, or same-net trace within tolerance. These span several functionally distinct nets: USB differential pair (/USB_DP, /USB_DM), crystal lines (/XTAL_IN, /XTAL_OUT), and SWD programming/debug lines (/SWDIO, /SWCLK).

  • All six carry the same low severity and the same confidence of 0.4, which is below the 0.5 threshold — these should be treated as unverified and manually checked, not as confirmed defects. It's plausible some or all are false positives from endpoint-detection tolerance rather than actual unterminated stubs.
  • If genuine, stubs on /USB_DP and /USB_DM are worth checking first among this group, since USB differential signaling is more sensitive to impedance discontinuities than the crystal or SWD lines, though no signal-integrity issue was actually flagged for these nets (Signal Integrity subscore is 100).
  • The common recommendation across all six is consistent: verify the endpoint lands on a pad, then extend or remove the stub as needed.
